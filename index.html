About Me

Where technology meets creativity

I'm a current sophomore at the University of Michigan majoring in Computer Science with a minor in Art. I bring together the analytical world of programming with the creative realm of visual design. I believe that the best solutions come from thinking both logically and creatively.

I'm super passionate about learning and growing in both technology and art. I'm willing to learn anything and put my 100% into every opportunity that comes my way.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me sketching, painting, solving jigsaw puzzles, fly-fishing, or skiing. I love how art informs my approach to problem-solving and how programming allows me to bring my creative visions to life.
